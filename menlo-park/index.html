Before you go

Days out with kids rarely fall apart over the activity itself

  • Confirm before you drive

    Weekly sessions get cancelled and hours change. This site links straight to each venue's map listing, which is always current.

  • Dress for microclimates

    The coast can run twenty degrees colder than inland on the same afternoon. In the Bay Area, layers aren't optional.

  • Solve parking in advance

    Golden Gate Park, Lake Merritt and Muir Woods all punish arriving at noon. Come early, reserve ahead, or take transit.

  • Plan one thing, not three

    Most of these fill a half day. Leaving room for a nap and some nothing-in-particular beats cramming in another stop.
